What this changes

Budget decision guide

Revenue at risk is compared with the spend saved by a 20% reduction. It is marginal revenue, not contribution margin; channels with no surviving signal are unresolved, not automatically wasteful.

Experiment priorities

Priorities favor large amounts of raw association removed by the falsification correction. Test one source at a time across randomized matched geographies and record every credited destination.

Method and limitations

Model. Daily channel exposure is transformed with channel-specific geometric carryover and Hill saturation. Annual seasonality, country trends, promotional depth and gift-with-purchase are controlled. Country effects are partially pooled.

Counterfactual. Every cell estimates the change at one credited destination when one source is reduced by 20% over the latest 365 days. Negative values are retained; zero means the adjusted evidence did not survive, not that the true effect is exactly zero.

Falsification correction. Each source history is independently circular-shifted 50 times. The median fake-history effect is removed, the cell's empirical 95% false-signal threshold is subtracted, and Benjamini-Hochberg control is applied across all 168 cells.

What this report no longer does. It does not force halo into a zero-sum credit-transfer ledger, infer lifetime value, contribution margin or optimal budgets from unavailable data, or label predictive fit as causality. Randomized holdouts remain the standard required to upgrade these estimates.
